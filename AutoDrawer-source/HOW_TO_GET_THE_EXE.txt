GET YOUR SINGLE .EXE (no Python needed, ~5 minutes, free)

1. Go to github.com and sign up / log in (free).
2. Click the "+" (top right) -> "New repository". Name it "auto-drawer", keep it Public, click "Create repository".
3. Click "uploading an existing file". Unzip this folder first, then drag EVERYTHING
   inside it into the browser (including the ".github" folder). Click "Commit changes".
4. Click the "Actions" tab. If asked, click the green "I understand my workflows" button.
   Wait ~3 minutes for the yellow dot to turn into a green tick.
5. Go back to the "Code" tab -> on the right, click "Releases" -> "Auto Drawer (latest)".
6. Download AutoDrawer.exe. Double-click it. Done. Share that same link with anyone.

If ".github" doesn't upload: click "Add file" -> "Create new file", type
.github/workflows/build.yml as the name, and paste the contents of that file in.
